Frequently Asked Questions

Click a question below to see the answer.

How loud do you get?

Loud enough to energize the room, never so loud that your bartender can't take orders. We match the level to your space and your crowd — and we read the room as the night goes on.

Do you play patios and outdoor spaces?

Absolutely — weather permitting. Outdoor setups take a little extra time, so let us know when you book and we'll arrive early.

How long are your sets?

Typically 3–4 hours with short breaks, but we're flexible: two-hour happy hour sets, full Friday nights, or anything in between.

Do you take requests?

Yes — it's the best part of the night. If we know it, we'll play it. Your regulars will love it.

How far ahead should we book?

Weekends book out 4–8 weeks in advance, especially in summer. But always ask — weeknights and last-minute openings happen.
