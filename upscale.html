Frequently Asked Questions

Click a question below to see the answer.

What areas do you serve?

Southeastern Wisconsin, including Milwaukee, Waukesha, Ozaukee, Washington, and Racine counties. Private events beyond that area are absolutely possible — just ask.

What does an evening typically look like?

Most dinner venues book us for two or three 45-minute sets across the evening, with breaks timed to your dinner service. We're flexible and happy to follow your flow.

How do you handle volume in a dining room?

That's our specialty. Our compact system is sized to the room, and we set levels so guests at every table can hold a conversation comfortably. We'll do a sound check with your staff before service.

What's your setup requirement?

Keyboard, upright bass, and a compact professional sound system — roughly an 8' x 8' corner and one or two standard outlets. Setup and sound check take about 30 minutes.

How far in advance should we book?

Weekends fill 4–8 weeks out, and holiday season fills even earlier. Contact us as soon as you have dates in mind — we'll always try to accommodate.
