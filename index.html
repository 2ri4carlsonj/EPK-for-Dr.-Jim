// The duo

TWO GUYS.
ONE LOUD ACOUSTIC DUO.

Jim on keys and vocals. Rob on upright bass and vocals. Between them: decades of stage time, a setlist that swings from The Beatles to Johnny Cash to Neil Diamond, and a knack for reading a room.

One night it's a packed bar singing "Sweet Caroline" at the top of their lungs. The next it's a senior community singing along to the songs they grew up on. Same duo, different volume knob.

Nominated for a 2026 WAMI Award — and honestly, just honored to be nominated.
