Frequently Asked Questions

Click a question below to see the answer.

What areas do you serve?

We perform across Southeastern Wisconsin, including Milwaukee, Waukesha, Ozaukee, Washington, and Racine counties. We're happy to travel farther for the right community — just ask!

How long is a typical performance?

Most communities book us for a 60–90 minute set. We can adjust the length to fit your activity schedule, and we're flexible about breaks for resident needs.

Do you perform in memory care units?

Yes! We have extensive experience with memory care and dementia-friendly performances. We keep volumes gentle, choose highly familiar songs, and invite residents to sing along at their own pace.

What does your setup require?

Very little. We bring our own keyboard, upright bass, and compact sound system. All we need is a standard power outlet and roughly an 8' x 8' performance space. Setup takes about 20 minutes.

Can residents request songs?

Absolutely — requests are our favorite part! Share a song list with us ahead of time and we'll work as many favorites as we can into the set.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend booking 4–6 weeks ahead, especially for holiday seasons. That said, reach out anytime — we'll do our best to accommodate last-minute dates.
