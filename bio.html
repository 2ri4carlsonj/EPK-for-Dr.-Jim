// How this started

EDUCATED.
EXPERIENCED.
STILL NOT FAMOUS.

Jim is from Milwaukee. Rob is from Madison. On paper, that should have been a rivalry. In reality, it was just two college-educated musicians who'd spent decades in bands, taught way too many private lessons, and still genuinely love playing for a room full of strangers.

Jim went to Judson University — piano major, vocal chords included. Rob went to UW-Whitewater — bass in hand, low end in heart. Both have been in bands since they should have been doing homework.

They met the honest way: in a band literally called "Your Mom Band" out of Madison in 2019. Jim joined on keys. Rob was already holding down bass guitar, trying to make it sound respectable. They liked the harmony, liked the hang, and a couple years later thought — why not strip it down to piano, upright bass, and two guys who can actually sing?

JIM CARLSON

Milwaukee • Piano, Vocals • Judson University

Teaches piano privately (patiently), plays in Suzie and the Detonators out of Sheboygan, and can pull off Billy Joel without an apology. Knows every lyric you forgot you knew. Brings the keyboard, the high harmonies, and the dad jokes.

ROB

Madison • Upright Bass / Bass Guitar, Vocals • UW-Whitewater

Teaches bass privately, plays in several groups including Janna and the Junkyard Dawg, and owns an upright bass that is taller than your refrigerator. Brings the low end, the grit, and the reason people dance. Yes, he slaps it.

2019
Your Mom Band — Madison. Jim joins. Rob is already there on bass guitar. Immediate musical bromance.
2021-22
Duo shows start. No tracks. No net. Just piano, upright, and two mics. Bars, patios, senior communities — if there's a corner, they'll play it.
2026
Nominated for a WAMI Award (Wisconsin Area Music Industry). Still acting surprised about it. Still cheaper than therapy.
